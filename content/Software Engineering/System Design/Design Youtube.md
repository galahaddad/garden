
Youtube may seem like a simple media player but the stats for its usage and share of the internet are miraculous. Lets dig in.
## Baseline facts about Youtube

Circa 2020, Youtube sits as a major node of the internet that nearly all people have accessed in one point in their life, and for some almost daily. 
- Total number of monthly active users: 2 billion 
- Number of videos watched per day: 5 billion
- 73% of US adults use Youtube
- 50 million creators
- Ad revenue $15.1 B for 2019, up 336% from 2018
- Responsible for 37% mobile internet traffic
- Available in 80 languages
